Origin

What is Sato?

Isan · Sticky rice · Generations

Sato is Thailand's traditional fermented sticky rice drink.

Brewed in the farming communities of northeast Thailand, in Isan, for generations. Made from sticky rice, fermented slowly.

It belongs to Thailand the way sake belongs to Japan, soju to Korea, and tequila to Mexico.

Why we chose to make Sato

Thailand grows the world's best rice. Everyone knows this.

What most people don't know: Thailand also makes Sato, and has for centuries.

While sake got its glow-up in the age of premiumisation, Sato got stuck in time.

We believe

We believe Sato deserved better than being frozen in the past for a century.

We believe "traditional" doesn't mean "unchangeable." We believe every great drink started somewhere humble. We're a small team who looked at Sato and saw the potential everyone else missed.

We took a cart horse and turned it into a Tesla.

The Story

Our rice

We use GI-certified rice.

What is GI-certified rice?

A Geographical Indication protects agricultural products from a specific region. Think Champagne, Parmigiano Reggiano, Basmati. For Sato Sato, every batch starts with the highest-quality rice available.

The challenge.

Sato originates in Isan, but there's currently no commercial supply of GI-certified rice from Khon Kaen. For now, we source GI-certified rice from elsewhere in Thailand rather than compromise on quality.

The mission.

We're working with the Agricultural Chamber of Commerce in Khon Kaen to support local farmers toward GI certification: the first Sato brewed with GI-certified rice from the birthplace of Sato itself.

Arnat Apal

Arnat is a Bangkokian, born and bred on the east side of the city, with a passion for fermenting, brewing, and inventing. Completely self-taught, he'll have an idea on Monday to try something he's never done before. By Friday he's taught himself how to do it. New flavours, new enzymes, new products: he's always tinkering.

That passion is what birthed the earliest versions of Sato Sato. Working with Yann, a third-generation distiller, Arnat perfected the formula over a year of intense trials: more than 60 test batches before we landed on the final recipe. We learned that Sato is easy to make, but good, stable, consistent Sato is very hard. We work to improve our products every day, committed to producing only the very best and never cutting corners.
