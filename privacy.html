Privacy

What this site keeps.

If you confirm you are 20 or older, this browser tab remembers that until you close it. That note lives in session storage. We do not set a cookie for the age check.

The stock form does not send your details to our server. It opens your email app, addressed to satosatobkk@gmail.com, with the name, venue, city, and message you typed.

We do not load an analytics script unless a measurement id is configured for the site. Venue links leave this site for Instagram or Google Maps.
